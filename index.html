History

His Seishiro Nagi is one of the main characters in Blue Lock and the protagonist of the spin-off Blue Lock: Episode Nagi. At the beginning of the story, Nagi is a lazy high school student who prefers playing video games and avoids anything that requires effort. Although he has exceptional natural athletic ability, he has no interest in sports. His life changes when his classmate, Reo Mikage, notices his incredible talent and convinces him to try soccer. Despite having played for only about six months, Nagi's extraordinary ball control and first-touch skills earn him a place in the Blue Lock project, where Japan's best young strikers compete to become the nation's greatest forward. During the Blue Lock competition, Nagi quickly proves himself to be one of the most gifted players. Initially, he relies only on his natural talent and plays mainly to support Reo's dream of winning the World Cup. However, after experiencing defeat against Yoichi Isagi, Nagi realizes that talent alone is not enough to become the world's best striker. This loss inspires him to work harder, improve his skills, and discover his own passion for soccer. Throughout the series, Nagi grows from an unmotivated genius into a determined player who develops his own ambition and strives to become one of the best strikers in the world..
